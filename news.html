HEARTio milestones and my own research, newest first, followed by the papers, abstracts, and patents behind them. Jump to publications.

Milestones

  1. Jul 2026

    Joined Innovation Works' AlphaLab Health portfolio

    HEARTio was added to the AlphaLab Health portfolio at Innovation Works, Pittsburgh's Ben Franklin Technology Partner.

  2. Apr 2026

    Pitched at Life Sciences Future SW

    I took the stage in the Entrepreneur Arena at Life Sciences Pennsylvania's Life Sciences Future SW conference in Pittsburgh to pitch HEARTio to investors, collaborators, and regional life-sciences leaders.

  3. Mar 2026

    $4.25M seed financing to launch the ECGio pivotal study

    HEARTio closed a $4.25 million seed round backed by Intelligence Ventures, Audacious Capital, VU Venture Partners, LifeX, Bessel, and Shepherd Ventures to complete our pivotal study and submit ECGio to the FDA for clearance. Coverage in Cardiac Vascular News, HIT Consultant, and Tech Funding News.

  4. Mar 2026

    Third U.S. patent issued

    U.S. Patent No. 12,573,505, the third in our family covering a neural model for predicting coronary disease from ECG signals, issued March 10, 2026.

  5. Mar 2026

    First-author abstract in JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions

    With Utkars Jain, Michael Leasure, Veronica Covalesky, and Gary Mintz, I presented "The Optimal Interval for Coronary Artery Disease Assessment from a 12-Lead Digital ECG to the Coronary Angiogram: Implications for Diagnostic Test Accuracy", on how the gap between the ECG and the angiogram changes the measured accuracy of AI-ECG.

  6. Jan 2026

    Cross-validation study published in JACC: Advances

    "Use of Electrocardiograms to Identify Coronary Artery Disease: Cross-Validation of an Artificial Intelligence Model" reports 10-fold cross-validation and external validation of HEARTio's model for predicting angiographically significant coronary artery disease from a resting 12-lead ECG, with collaborators at Allegheny Health Network, Jefferson, Temple, and Pitt.

  7. Oct 2025

    Pittsburgh Tomorrow "Pioneers" interview

    Utkars and I sat down with Pittsburgh Tomorrow for their Pioneers video series to share HEARTio's journey from a student idea to ECGio.

  8. Oct 2025

    Selected for the HeartX Cardiovascular Accelerator

    HEARTio was one of seven companies chosen for the 2025 HeartX Cardiovascular Accelerator, run by HealthTech Arkansas with MedAxiom and the American College of Cardiology, which pairs each startup with a guaranteed hospital pilot or clinical study.

  9. Oct 2025

    TCT 2025: a PET-CT verified AI algorithm for ischemia

    We presented "A PET-CT Verified AI Algorithm to Predict the Ischemic Impact of Coronary Artery Disease" (TCT-898) at TCT 2025, published in the Journal of the American College of Cardiology supplement.

  10. Sep 2025

    Presented at Life Sciences Future 2025

    I presented HEARTio's AI-powered ECG diagnostics for coronary artery disease at Life Sciences Future 2025, Life Sciences Pennsylvania's annual innovation conference.

  11. Mar 2025

    ECGio at CRT 2025

    My cofounder Michael Leasure presented "ECGio: AI-Based Diagnostics, More Quickly, More Accurately, and at a Fraction of the Cost" in the Innovations in Artificial Intelligence session at CRT 2025 in Washington, DC.

  12. Jan 2025

    First-author paper in IEEE/ACM Transactions on Computational Biology and Bioinformatics

    "Automating Knowledge-Driven Model Recommendation: Methodology, Evaluation, and Key Challenges", written with Niloofar Arazkhani, Cheryl Telmer, and Natasa Miskov-Zivanov, describes how to automatically assemble, extend, and evaluate executable models of biological signaling networks from machine-read literature. First posted as an arXiv preprint in January 2023.

  13. Oct 2024

    TCT 2024: the ROPES study

    We presented "Artificial Intelligence Assessment of a Digital 12 Lead ECG Predicts Clinically Significant CAD With a Diagnostic Accuracy Approaching 90%: The ROPES Study" (TCT-197) at TCT 2024.

  14. Aug 2024

    AI-derived fractional flow reserve from the ECG

    With collaborators at the National Institute of Cardiology in Warsaw, we posted a preprint on using AI analysis of the ECG to estimate fractional flow reserve (FFR), a measure of how much a coronary blockage actually limits blood flow.

  15. Jul 2024

    Second U.S. patent issued

    U.S. Patent No. 12,040,093, a continuation covering our neural model for predicting coronary disease from ECG signals, issued July 16, 2024 (the application was published in June 2023).

  16. Apr 2024

    Ph.D. conferred

    I was awarded my Ph.D. in Bioengineering by the University of Pittsburgh in April 2024, after seven years in the MeLoDy Lab with Natasa Miskov-Zivanov. My dissertation, "Automated and Manual Assembly of Computational Models for Biological Systems: Applications to Macrophage Activation and CAR-T Cells", was published in Pitt's repository that June.

  17. Apr 2024

    A Big Idea Competition springboard

    Six years after our first pitch, Pitt's Office of Innovation and Entrepreneurship listed HEARTio among the notable student and alumni startups that used the Big Idea Competition as a springboard.

  18. Nov 2023

    Forbes 30 Under 30, Healthcare

    Recognized alongside my cofounders Utkars Jain and Michael Leasure on the Forbes 30 Under 30 Healthcare list for 2024. Coverage from Technical.ly, Pittwire, and Villanova Engineering.

  19. 2023

    FDA sign-off on pivotal study protocol

    The FDA signed off on our pivotal clinical study protocol with no additional comments in the meeting, clearing the path to the study that supports market authorization.

  20. Jun 2023

    Richard King Mellon Foundation investment

    The Richard King Mellon Foundation invested $300,000 in HEARTio through its Social-Impact Investment program, backing our AI-powered ECG diagnostic as part of its Health & Well-Being portfolio.

  21. May 2023

    Reconstructing a 12-lead ECG from fewer leads

    We published "Importance of Electrode Selection and Number in Reconstructing Standard Twelve Lead Electrocardiograms" in Biomedicines, showing how many leads, and which ones, you really need to rebuild a full 12-lead ECG.

  22. May 2023

    SNAP-CAR receptors in Nature Communications

    During my PhD under Dr. Natasa Miskov-Zivanov, I was lucky enough to support the great work of Dr. Jason Lohmueller and his CAR and synNotch receptors for programmable antigen targeting. I created a mathematical model describing his work and was included in the Nature Communications paper. Pitt's Swanson School covered the work.

  23. Mar 2023

    First place and $75,000, Tulane Business Model Competition

    Selected from 123 applications, 6 semi-finalists, and 3 finalists. Thank you to Tulane University and the Albert Lepage Center for Entrepreneurship and Innovation at the A. B. Freeman School of Business. New Orleans was an amazing time. Tulane's write-up.

  24. Jan 2023

    First U.S. patent issued

    U.S. Patent No. 11,568,991, "Medical diagnostic tool with neural model trained through machine learning for predicting coronary disease from ECG signals," issued January 31, 2023.

The same year we presented the results at the European Society of Cardiology Congress, TCT, and the American Heart Association Scientific Sessions. Press release.
